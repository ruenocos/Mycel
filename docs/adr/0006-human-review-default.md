# 0006 Human review by default

**Status:** accepted, 13 Nov 2026

**Decision:** autopilot level 1: AI drafts, a human approves and publishes. Nothing writes to a website in the MVP. Before the first live change (phase 5): kill switches on publishing and autopilot, and a saved previous version of anything changed.

**Why:** wrong AI content on a client site is the costliest failure; trust comes before automation.
