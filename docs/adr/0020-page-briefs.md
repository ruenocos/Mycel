# 0020 Page briefs: a structured brief per map page, facts only from the profile

**Status:** accepted, 22 Nov 2026. Builds on 0005, 0006, 0012, 0013 and 0014. The fields follow the page design Wons approved for the app's Brief section (modelled on Frase's brief).

**Decision:** `mycel brief <topical-map.json>` writes one brief per map page to `briefs/<node id>.json` next to the map. The file is `Brief` in `packages/schema/src/brief.ts`, with `version: 2` (1 was the plain-text brief of the writing test, #12).

- **What a brief holds:** the core message; the reader (what they search for, how they feel, what they do next); what makes the page different from the current top results, with those results; the evidence (facts with their source and a check state); the outline per heading level (heading, purpose, form, points, evidence used, words); angles to avoid; questions to answer; internal links to and from other map pages; the call to action with the business's conversion; targets (words, H2/H3 count, uses of the main search, reading level B1); the domain terms to use; and the research it was made from (the map's `runId` and `createdAt`), so a reader can tell the research changed since.
- **Data first, the model writes around it.** Questions (people also ask, related searches, question searches, competitors' gap topics), links (the map's own links, both ways), the top results, the targets and the facts are cut from the files the pipeline already wrote. The model writes the message, reader, difference, outline, angles and CTA, and picks evidence, questions and anchors by number. It adds nothing to those lists.
- **Facts come only from the business profile** (facts, differentiators, the page's offering, places served), each with its source: the site page the profile cites, else the profile field (`facts.0`, `offerings.<id>.priceRange`). Quoted as the profile has them. Every fact starts `unchecked`; a person checks it (`checked`, by, at). Re-running keeps a check when fact and source stay the same, and a brief with checked facts is only replaced with `--force`.
- **The checks** after the model: evidence ids it wasn't shown are dropped; an outline point that names a number no evidence, search or question has, or fails the map's fact check (0017: places, prices, praise, not-offered, jargon), is dropped; any other text that fails is kept and listed in `warnings` for the person. The outline is put in shape (one H1 first, H3 under H2) and the word targets are scaled to the page's total.
- **Language:** English prompt, every text in the market language (0013). Searches, questions and titles stay as found.
- **Money:** one model call per page (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on Ollama Cloud, 0012), retried once when the answer can't make a brief. Results pages come from research.json and the DataforSEO cache only; a brief never buys a SERP. The LLM client got a cost cap (`--budget`, default $0.25): a call is refused when what was spent plus its prompt would pass it. It binds for a priced model (`LLM_PRICE_USD_PER_MTOK`); Ollama Cloud is a subscription with no price per call. `--dry-run` counts the tokens per page and the cost before anything is called.
- **Status:** `brief` is the last step in `mycel status`: missing until the folder has a brief, stale when a brief is older than the map or business profile, or an older step version wrote it.

**Why:** the map says which pages to make; a writer (or the next model call) needs the rest of what a content brief tool gives, tied to this business's confirmed facts instead of the model's memory. A file per page keeps a re-run of one page from touching the others and lets the app read one page's brief.

**Not yet:** facts from cited pages outside the profile (a source fetcher), word targets from the competitors' own word counts, an eval for briefs like `mycel eval map`.
